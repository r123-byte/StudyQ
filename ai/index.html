AI transparency

StudyQ AI features are optional. Do not submit passwords, confidential school records, or sensitive information about yourself or others.

Study planner

The browser sends your prompt and selected study context through StudyQ's Vercel API proxy to Groq. The configured chat model is openai/gpt-oss-120b.

Practice Test

PDF text extraction and scanned-page OCR run in your browser. When you ask to generate a test, extracted text and test settings are sent to StudyQ's dedicated Vercel Practice Test endpoint, which calls Groq using openai/gpt-oss-120b. The PDF file and page images are not sent in that generation request.

Voice dictation

When you activate dictation, the browser records audio and sends the recording through StudyQ's proxy to Groq for transcription with whisper-large-v3. The code limits recordings to 120 seconds.

Answer grading

Open-answer questions, marking criteria and your answers are sent through StudyQ's proxy to OpenRouter. The proxy restricts the model to liquid/lfm-3-1.7b:free.

Storage and provider practices

Some resulting study information is saved in your browser. StudyQ has no central student account database in this codebase. Provider retention, model-training use, processing locations and deletion schedules have not been verified from the current provider account settings. Do not assume submitted information is automatically deleted or processed only in Europe.

AI outputs can be inaccurate and should be checked. See the Privacy Policy and Cookies and browser storage.
