Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Short version: StudyStack is designed with privacy in mind. You don't need an account to use the tools, and the vast majority of your data (like your grades, flashcards, and study plans) never leaves your device.

1. Data Storage (Local Storage)

StudyStack is a static web application. When you use our tools to add tests, create flashcards, or calculate grades, that data is saved directly in your browser using Local Storage.

  • This data is physically stored on your device.
  • We do not have servers that store or process this data.
  • Clearing your browser's cache or site data will permanently delete your saved progress.

2. Third-Party Services

While we do not store your personal data, we do use a few external services for specific features:

  • AI Planner (Nova): When you chat with the AI planner, your prompts and study data are sent to our secure proxy and then to OpenRouter to generate a response. We do not log or store these conversations.
  • Voice Input: If you use the microphone feature in the planner, your audio is securely processed by Groq to transcribe it to text. Audio is not stored.
  • Contact Form: If you contact us via the Contact page, your message is processed by Web3Forms and sent directly to our email inbox.

3. Cookies & Tracking

We do not use tracking cookies, analytics scripts, or advertising pixels. We do not track your behavior across the internet or build profiles about you.

4. Your Rights

Because we don't store your personal data on our servers, you have complete control over it. You can manage or delete your data at any time by clearing your browser's Local Storage.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, or if you've found a bug or have a feature idea, please reach out to us via our Contact Page.
