Cookies and browser storage

Last reviewed: 15 November 2026

Source review found no first-party code that reads or writes cookies. This does not establish whether hosting or third-party services set cookies in a live browser; production storage inspection is still required.

Local storage

StudyQ saves settings and study information in browser localStorage, including language, navigation preference, flashcards, vocabulary sessions, planner items, practice-test records and audio preferences. These are browser storage entries, not cookies. They generally remain until removed by a feature or cleared in browser site-data settings. Clearing them may delete saved study work.

Session storage

The homepage uses sessionStorage for temporary navigation return information under StudyQ-prefixed keys. Older key names migrate and are removed when the homepage script runs. This is session-scoped and is not a cookie.

Analytics and external assets

StudyQ loads Umami analytics automatically on selected pages through the same-origin /stats/ route. The tracker is configured to omit URL query strings and fragments and to disable tracking when a supported browser Do Not Track signal is enabled. This setting is not a consent choice and does not control hosting or network logs. The deployed tracker version, account settings and retention have not been independently verified. Google Fonts and selected PDF/OCR libraries are also loaded from external providers.

Manage browser data

The Privacy Policy page can clear StudyQ study content and history, or all StudyQ-prefixed browser storage. It does not clear unrelated sites’ storage or information already sent to external providers.

You can clear StudyQ site data in your browser settings. This removes local StudyQ information and may remove saved work. It does not delete data already transmitted to external services. Contact hello.studyq@gmail.com with privacy requests.
