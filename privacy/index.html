1. Introduction

StudyQ is a free educational platform providing students with digital tools for studying, revision, learning and organisation.

This Privacy Policy explains what information StudyQ processes, why it is processed, how it is stored, which third-party services are involved, how long information may be retained, and what rights visitors have under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and applicable European privacy laws.

StudyQ aims to minimise the personal information it collects and processes.

3. Personal Data We Process

StudyQ does not require visitors to create accounts to access its main study tools.

Depending on the features used, the following information may be processed.

3.1 Browser-stored information

StudyQ uses browser storage to save information and preferences on the visitor's device.

This may include:

  • Language and website preferences.
  • Vocabulary decks and learning progress.
  • Flashcards and flashcard progress.
  • Test-week schedules and school planning information.
  • Calculator preferences and related information.
  • AI Planner conversation history.
  • Practice Test history.
  • Temporary navigation and session information.

This information is generally stored in localStorage or sessionStorage.

StudyQ does not operate a central user account database for this study information.

Visitors can remove their stored StudyQ information using the website's deletion controls or their browser settings. Deletion may permanently remove saved study information.

3.2 Technical information

When visitors access StudyQ, its hosting and infrastructure providers may process technical information required to deliver, secure and maintain the website.

This may include:

  • IP addresses.
  • Browser and device information.
  • Requested URLs and resources.
  • Request timestamps.
  • Error and diagnostic information.
  • Security and abuse-prevention information.

The exact information and retention depend on the relevant provider and service configuration.

3.3 Contact form information

When visitors use the StudyQ contact form, information submitted may include:

  • Name, if provided.
  • Email address.
  • Message.
  • Other information voluntarily included.

The contact form is processed through Web3Forms.

Visitors should not include passwords, sensitive personal information or confidential school information in contact messages.

3.4 AI feature information

When visitors use AI-powered features, information they submit may be transmitted to external AI providers.

Depending on the feature, this may include:

  • Questions and prompts.
  • Extracted study material.
  • Instructions for generating practice tests.
  • Conversation context.
  • Voice recordings submitted for transcription.
  • Answers submitted for AI-assisted grading.

This information is processed to provide the requested AI functionality.

StudyQ does not intentionally sell submitted study material or use it to create advertising profiles.

3.5 Rate-limiting information

StudyQ uses Upstash Redis to enforce usage limits and help prevent abuse of AI features.

This system may process:

  • Pseudonymous identifiers derived from IP addresses using keyed HMAC.
  • Request timestamps.
  • Temporary request identifiers.
  • Usage counters and reservation information.

The rate-limiting implementation is designed not to store AI prompts, uploaded documents, extracted study material or voice recordings.

6. Cookies and Browser Storage

6.1 Cookies

StudyQ does not intentionally set traditional cookies for its core website functionality.

StudyQ does not use advertising cookies or behavioural advertising profiles.

The Umami analytics tracker used by StudyQ is configured for basic, cookieless analytics.

Third-party infrastructure may still process technical request information independently of cookies.

6.2 localStorage

StudyQ uses localStorage to retain information between visits.

Examples include:

  • Settings and preferences.
  • Vocabulary decks.
  • Flashcards.
  • Study progress.
  • Test-week planning information.

This information generally remains on the visitor's device until deleted by the visitor or browser.

6.3 sessionStorage

StudyQ uses sessionStorage for temporary information required during a browsing session.

This information is generally removed when the relevant browser session or tab ends.

6.4 Managing browser storage

Visitors can clear StudyQ information through the website's deletion controls or browser settings.

Clearing browser storage may permanently remove saved study information and preferences.

Browser storage is technically different from cookies. However, EU ePrivacy requirements can apply to both, depending on their purpose and implementation.

7. Website Analytics – Umami

StudyQ uses Umami Cloud's free Hobby plan to understand general website usage and improve the platform.

Analytics is loaded on the following pages:

  • Homepage.
  • About.
  • Calculator.
  • Contact.
  • Cookies.
  • Flashcards.
  • Planner.
  • Pomodoro.
  • Practice Test.
  • Vocabulary Trainer.

The analytics integration uses the same-origin /stats/ route.

The implementation is configured to:

  • Exclude URL query parameters and fragments.
  • Respect the browser's Do Not Track preference where supported.
  • Avoid intentionally sending study content or personal form contents to analytics.

Umami's standard tracker does not use cookies. It generates anonymous visitor identifiers using technical information such as the website identifier, IP address, browser information and a rotating salt.

The raw IP address is not stored as a field in standard Umami analytics records. However, IP addresses may be processed temporarily and may appear in separate operational or security logs.

Analytics retention

StudyQ uses the Umami Cloud Hobby plan.

The published analytics retention period for the Hobby plan is 6 months.

Relevant provider information:

  • Privacy Policy: https://umami.is/privacy
  • Cloud FAQ: https://docs.umami.is/docs/cloud/faq

StudyQ does not use analytics to create advertising profiles or sell visitor data.

8.2 Groq – AI processing

Provider: Groq

Website: https://groq.com/

Groq processes information submitted through StudyQ's AI-powered features, including:

  • AI Planner.
  • Practice Test generation.
  • Voice transcription.

Depending on the feature, submitted information may include prompts, extracted study material, conversation context and audio recordings.

#### Published retention information

Groq states that inference inputs and outputs are not retained by default.

Groq may temporarily retain customer inputs and outputs for system reliability or abuse monitoring for up to 30 days, unless a longer period is legally required.

Usage metadata is collected for service activity and performance. This metadata does not include customer inputs or outputs.

Groq provides a Zero Data Retention option in its account Data Controls settings. This is a separate setting and should not be assumed to be enabled.

StudyQ does not use Groq batch processing or fine-tuning functionality as part of its described active AI flows.

Relevant provider information:

8.3 OpenRouter – AI-assisted grading

Provider: OpenRouter

Website: https://openrouter.ai/

OpenRouter is used for AI-assisted grading.

Information submitted for grading may be transmitted through OpenRouter to the underlying AI model provider.

#### Published retention information

OpenRouter states that it does not store prompt and response content by default unless input/output logging is enabled.

If input/output logging is enabled, OpenRouter's documentation states that logged content is retained for a minimum of three months and may be retained longer unless deletion is requested.

OpenRouter also stores request metadata, such as token counts, latency and model information. A universal retention period for this metadata is not specified in the cited documentation.

The underlying model provider may have separate retention and training policies. These policies vary by provider and model endpoint.

OpenRouter provides provider-level data policy controls, including zero-retention and training restrictions. The actual provider selected for a request matters.

Relevant provider information:

  • Privacy Policy: https://openrouter.ai/privacy
  • Data Collection: https://github.com/OpenRouterTeam/docs/blob/main/guides/privacy/data-collection.mdx
  • Provider Data Policies: https://openrouter.ai/providers

Visitors should avoid submitting sensitive personal information through AI grading features.

8.4 Upstash – Rate limiting and temporary data

Provider: Upstash

Plan: Free

Website: https://upstash.com/

StudyQ uses Upstash Redis for server-side rate limiting and abuse prevention.

The system processes pseudonymous identifiers, temporary request information, timestamps and usage counters.

The application is designed not to store AI prompts, uploaded documents, extracted study material or voice recordings in the rate-limiting database.

#### StudyQ application retention

StudyQ uses rolling hourly usage limits.

Rate-limit counters, completed usage records and temporary pending reservations can remain counted for up to the full 60-minute window. A reservation may be released sooner when a generation fails and the storage service is reachable. Reservations whose completion status cannot be confirmed remain counted for the hourly window.

#### Upstash provider retention

Upstash states that it retains user personal information for as long as an account is active or as needed to provide its services.

The published audit-log retention period for the Free plan is 7 days.

This is Upstash account audit-log retention, not the expiry period of StudyQ's Redis rate-limit records.

Relevant provider information:

  • Privacy Policy: https://upstash.com/trust/privacy
  • Audit Logs: https://upstash.com/docs/common/account/auditlogs
  • Backup Documentation: https://upstash.com/docs/redis/features/backup

9. AI Features and Study Material

StudyQ includes AI-powered functionality to support learning.

9.1 Practice Test generator

Visitors may submit study material to generate practice tests.

Where documents are processed in the browser, text extraction or OCR may take place locally before relevant extracted text is sent to Groq.

The active Practice Test flow is designed to send extracted text rather than the original PDF file.

Practice Test history may be stored locally in the browser. Source material is excluded from persisted Practice Test history.

9.2 AI Planner

The AI Planner may process prompts and relevant conversation context to generate study-planning suggestions.

Conversation history may be stored locally in the browser.

9.3 Voice transcription

Voice recordings submitted through the voice functionality are transmitted to Groq for transcription.

9.4 AI grading

Submitted answers may be transmitted through OpenRouter to an underlying model provider for grading.

9.5 AI limitations

AI-generated information may be inaccurate, incomplete or misleading.

Visitors should independently check important educational information.

Sensitive personal information, passwords and confidential information should not be submitted through AI features.
