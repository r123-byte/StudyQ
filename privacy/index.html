Privacy Policy — StudyTools

Effective date: 18 October 2026

Last updated: 19 October 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to StudyTools ("we", "us" or "our"), an educational website providing study tools such as flashcards, vocabulary training, grade calculation, study planning and productivity features.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is stored, used and transmitted when you use StudyTools, including its AI planner, voice dictation, contact form and Magister calendar import feature.

We aim to minimise personal data processing and explain our practices transparently. Where applicable, this policy is intended to address the requirements of the General Data Protection Regulation (EU) 2016/679 ("GDPR") and relevant Dutch privacy laws.

StudyTools does not require users to create an account. Most saved study information is stored locally in your browser. Some features require information to be transmitted to external service providers.

3. Information Stored Locally in Your Browser

StudyTools uses browser `localStorage` to save information needed for its features.

Depending on how you use the website, this information may include:

  • **Preferences:** Selected language, sound preferences and other settings.
  • **Flashcards:** Custom decks, vocabulary items and learning progress.
  • **Study planning:** Tests, subjects, homework tasks and schedules.
  • **Study progress:** Completed tasks, checklists and progress information.
  • **Planner history:** A limited history of previous study interactions.
  • **AI-generated schedules:** Schedules and other planner results saved for later use.
  • **Temporary application data:** Information used to prepare, transfer or display results.

This information is generally stored in your browser rather than in a central StudyTools user database.

Some locally stored information may constitute personal data if it relates to an identifiable person. For example, a study plan may include identifying information entered by a user.

Local storage and deletion

Locally stored information generally remains on your device until it is removed by a feature, cleared through your browser settings or otherwise deleted.

Clearing the relevant website data may permanently remove saved flashcards, schedules, preferences and study progress. StudyTools cannot recover locally stored information after deletion unless another copy exists.

Browser local storage is not an encrypted vault. Other people who can access your device or browser profile may be able to access it.

Please avoid storing passwords, confidential information or unnecessary sensitive personal data in StudyTools.

4. Information Processed by External Services

Some StudyTools features require information to be sent to external services. This occurs when you actively use the relevant feature or submit information.

4.1 AI study planner — Vercel and OpenRouter

When you use the AI study planner, the website sends the relevant prompt and study context to an API proxy hosted on Vercel. The proxy forwards the request to OpenRouter for AI processing.

Depending on your request and the feature used, the information transmitted may include:

  • Instructions and prompts you provide.
  • Test dates, subjects and homework information included in the request.
  • Relevant study schedules and progress information.
  • Previous study interactions included as context.

This information is used to generate study-related responses and planning suggestions.

Vercel hosts the API proxy. OpenRouter may route requests to a selected AI model provider. These services may process request data and technical information to provide, secure and operate their services.

StudyTools does not intentionally maintain a central database of AI conversations. This does not guarantee that Vercel, OpenRouter or a selected model provider never retains request data or technical logs.

Further information:

  • [Vercel Privacy Notice](https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-notice)
  • [OpenRouter Privacy Policy](https://openrouter.ai/privacy/)
  • [OpenRouter Provider Information](https://openrouter.ai/providers)

4.2 Voice dictation — Groq

When you activate voice dictation, your audio is transmitted to the Groq API for speech-to-text processing using a Whisper model.

The resulting transcription may then be used by the relevant StudyTools feature. If you submit that transcription to the AI planner, the text and relevant study context may also be sent through the API proxy for AI processing.

Voice dictation is initiated by the user. Audio is not intentionally transmitted through this feature when it has not been activated.

The handling and retention of audio, transcriptions and associated technical information depend on Groq's applicable terms, privacy practices and data-handling settings. We do not guarantee that all audio or transcription data is immediately deleted after processing.

Further information:

  • [Groq Privacy Policy](https://groq.com/privacy-policy/)
  • [Groq Documentation](https://console.groq.com/docs/your-data)

4.3 Contact form — Web3Forms

If you submit a message through the StudyTools Contact page, the information you provide is transmitted to Web3Forms for processing and delivery to the configured recipient.

This may include your name, email address, enquiry category and message.

The information is used to receive and respond to your enquiry. Web3Forms and the recipient may process or retain submissions in accordance with their applicable policies and arrangements.

Submitting a contact form is a separate action from using the study tools.

Further information: [Web3Forms](https://web3forms.com/).

4.4 Magister calendar import — iCalendar and `webcal` links

StudyTools allows users to paste a personal Magister iCalendar (`iCal`) or `webcal` link to import calendar information into the study planner.

#### Information processed

The calendar link may provide access to information such as lesson times and schedule entries. The available information depends on the calendar feed provided by Magister.

Based on the current implementation, the calendar link is processed in the browser and is not sent to the StudyTools API proxy. The browser uses the link to retrieve the calendar feed directly from the relevant calendar service.

The calendar service may receive technical information associated with the request, such as the user's IP address and request details.

Calendar information retrieved by the browser is processed locally by StudyTools unless another feature or network request transmits it elsewhere. StudyTools does not intentionally send the Magister calendar link or imported calendar data to its API proxy.

#### Confidentiality of your calendar link

A personal Magister calendar link should be treated as confidential because someone who obtains a functioning link may be able to access the calendar information it exposes.

Only provide the link if you choose to use the calendar-import feature. Avoid sharing it publicly or sending it to people you do not trust.

If the link is stored in your browser, it may remain there until you remove it or clear the relevant website data.

#### Revoking access

Magister provides controls for managing calendar sharing. If you believe your calendar link has been exposed, use the available Magister controls to stop sharing or generate a replacement link.

Revoking or replacing a link may prevent future access through the old link. It does not automatically delete calendar data that may already have been retrieved or retained elsewhere.

Further information: [Magister Support](https://service.magister.nl/).

5. Cookies, Local Storage and Similar Technologies

StudyTools currently does not intentionally use advertising cookies or analytics tracking technologies.

Based on our current checks, no cookies were visible in the website's Chrome Application panel. However, this does not guarantee that no cookies or similar technologies are ever set by the website, its features or external services.

StudyTools uses `localStorage` to retain information required by its features. Local storage is technically different from cookies, but laws governing the storage of information on, or access to information from, a user's device may also apply.

We aim to use device storage only for purposes connected to the website's functionality.

If we introduce cookies or similar technologies that require consent under applicable law, we will implement the required consent mechanism before using them.

Users can manage or clear locally stored information through their browser settings. Doing so may remove saved StudyTools data.

6. Purposes and Legal Bases for Processing

Where the GDPR applies, personal data must be processed on a valid legal basis under Article 6 GDPR.

The appropriate legal basis depends on the actual processing activity and circumstances. The following table describes potential bases that must be assessed and confirmed by the data controller.

| Activity | Purpose | Potential legal basis |

|---|---|---|

| Local browser storage | Saving preferences and study information | Applicable device-storage rules and, where relevant, an appropriate GDPR basis |

| AI planner | Generating study plans and responses | Article 6(1)(b) where necessary for a contract, or Article 6(1)(a) where valid consent is appropriate |

| Voice dictation | Converting speech into text | An appropriate basis under Article 6 GDPR |

| Magister calendar import | Retrieving and organising calendar information | An appropriate basis under Article 6 GDPR |

| Contact form | Receiving and responding to enquiries | Article 6(1)(b) for relevant pre-contractual requests, or Article 6(1)(f) where legitimate interests are appropriate |

| Technical security information | Operating, maintaining and securing the service | Article 6(1)(f), where a legitimate-interest assessment supports its use |

| Legal compliance | Meeting applicable legal obligations | Article 6(1)(c), where processing is necessary for a legal obligation |

These are potential legal bases, not a declaration that every listed basis currently applies. The data controller must determine and document the appropriate basis for each actual processing activity.

Where consent is required, it must be freely given, specific, informed and unambiguous. Consent may be withdrawn at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.

Where processing relies on legitimate interests, those interests and the required balancing assessment must be documented.

Rules governing cookies and other technologies that store or access information on a user's device must also be assessed separately from the GDPR legal basis.

9. Data Retention

Retention depends on the type of information, the feature used and the service processing it.

  • **Local browser data:** Generally remains until deleted by the user, removed by a feature or cleared through browser settings.
  • **AI planner requests:** May be subject to processing, logging or retention by Vercel, OpenRouter and the selected model provider.
  • **Voice recordings and transcriptions:** Subject to Groq's applicable data-handling practices and settings, as well as any subsequent processing of the transcription.
  • **Contact form submissions:** May be retained by Web3Forms or the configured recipient for as long as necessary to handle the enquiry and meet applicable obligations.
  • **Magister calendar links and imported data:** Processed in the browser according to the calendar feature's implementation. The calendar service may retain request logs under its applicable practices.
  • **Technical logs:** May be retained by hosting or infrastructure providers for operational, security or legal purposes.

StudyTools does not guarantee immediate deletion of information from third-party systems after a request has been processed.

Where the data controller determines the retention period for personal data under its control, information will be retained only for as long as necessary for the relevant purpose, unless applicable law requires otherwise.

10. Data Security

We aim to protect information processed through StudyTools by minimising unnecessary processing and applying appropriate technical and organisational measures.

No method of transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Your Rights Under the GDPR

Where the GDPR applies, you may have the following rights, subject to the conditions and limitations established by law:

  • **Right of access — Article 15:** To obtain confirmation of whether your personal data is being processed and access to that data and relevant information.
  • **Right to rectification — Article 16:** To request correction of inaccurate personal data or completion of incomplete data.
  • **Right to erasure — Article 17:** To request deletion of personal data where the applicable conditions are met.
  • **Right to restriction — Article 18:** To request restriction of processing in the circumstances specified by law.
  • **Right to data portability — Article 20:** To receive eligible personal data in a structured, commonly used and machine-readable format and, where applicable, transmit it to another controller.
  • **Right to object — Article 21:** To object to processing based on Article 6(1)(e) or Article 6(1)(f) on grounds relating to your particular situation, subject to the applicable legal requirements.
  • **Right to withdraw consent:** Where processing is based on consent, to withdraw it at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.

You may exercise your rights by contacting the data controller using the details in Section 2.

The data controller will respond within the period required by Article 12 GDPR, normally within one month. Where legally permitted, this period may be extended for complex or multiple requests. You will be informed of any extension and the reasons for it.

Where information is stored solely in your browser, you can generally delete it yourself through the relevant feature, where available, or through your browser's site-data settings.

Where information is processed independently by a third-party provider, you may also need to contact that provider directly. Revoking a Magister calendar link can stop future access through that link, but it does not automatically erase copies of information already retrieved.
